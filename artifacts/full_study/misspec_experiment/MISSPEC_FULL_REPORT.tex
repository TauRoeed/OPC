%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\phonesection{0. Teaser (read after Sec.~5 for numbers)}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

We build synthetic click worlds whose true logit
contains a controlled residual field $\phi$,
constructed to be near-orthogonal to
\emph{clean} bilinear structure (measured
linear visibility on the learner's
\emph{biased} features is $\approx 0$).
Learners that maximize Bernoulli likelihood
(NLL) therefore converge toward a different
in-class policy than learners that target
decision value via a doubly-robust objective
(DR~/~OPC).  A hybrid schedule
$f_{\star}{=}0.5$ (NLL then DR fine-tune) is
the locked compromise.

DiD $=D(\gamma)-D(0)$ with level
$D=V_{\mathrm{OPC}}-V_{\mathrm{NLL}}$;
abs CTR and DiD are different objects
(full tables in Sec.~7--7d; CI $=$ paired-seed
$t$ interval).

\textbf{Headline numbers}:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Primary pre-registered:
        $\Rstar$ at $\gamma^{\star}{=}0.5$,
        OPC$-$NLL DiD
        $=+1.53$\,pt $[+0.97,+2.09]$;
        $10/10$ (ML). Abs: OPC $18.42$,
        NLL $17.96$ at $\gamma^{\star}$.
  \item Hybrid $f{=}0.5$ (ML, $n{=}20$):
        best abs CTR $19.62$ at $\gamma^{\star}$;
        Hy$-$NLL DiD $+1.19$ $[0.59,1.79]$.
  \item SoftAND/SoftThresh: mid-$\gamma$
        OPC$-$NLL DiD null; $\gamma{=}100$
        large clear DiDs ($+3.8$ to $+7.2$).
  \item Capacity (Sec.~7c): OPC abs $V$
        rises with $N$; $N^{\star}$ advances
        with width. Mild-$\gamma$ ``OPC loses''
        is NLL catch-up.
  \item Scaling exponents (Sec.~8): still
        blocked on A1 / NLL-floor pin; method
        unchanged.
\end{itemize}

\why{Oracle gaps bound what is possible;
they do not predict what finite-sample
learners realize.  Always ask whether a
quoted number is a \emph{level} $D(\gamma)$
or a \emph{DiD} $D(\gamma)-D(0)$.}

\phonesection{0b. Glossary}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\begin{itemize}
  \item $\mathcal{F}$ --- locked policy-score
        class (affine-bilinear in biased
        embeddings unless a width $C$ is
        stated).  ``Misspec'' is always
        relative to $\mathcal{F}$.
  \item Pseudo-true / KL projection ---
        the member of $\mathcal{F}$ that
        infinite-data NLL converges to; it
        depends on the pair distribution
        (logging measure).
  \item OPC --- the repo's DR-based
        \emph{policy-learning} objective
        (DM$+$IPS); used interchangeably
        with ``DR'' in this note.
  \item Hy$_f$ --- NLL for a fraction $f$
        of epochs, then DR fine-tuning
        from those weights.
  \item $\ell_{\gamma}$ --- true click logit
        of the simulator (DGP).
  \item $\phi$ --- residual field; family
        chooses geometry, $\gamma$ chooses
        loudness.
  \item $\pi_{0}$ --- logging policy
        (known; temperature frozen at
        $\gamma{=}0$).
  \item $V(\pi)$ --- exact-greedy CTR under
        true $q_{\gamma}$ (simulator oracle,
        not an OPE estimator).
  \item $\texttt{signal\_kept}$ --- fraction
        of clean-embedding signal remaining
        in biased features ($\approx 0.5$ in
        the locked \texttt{high} cell).
  \item Double-centering --- remove user
        and item main effects from $\phi$
        so affine terms in $\mathcal{F}$
        cannot soak them up.
  \item PERP --- subtract the best clean
        affine-bilinear fit to
        $\phi_{\mathrm{raw}}$ under uniform
        catalog pairs.
  \item Unit-sd / \texttt{norm=v2} ---
        rescale $\phi$ to SD 1 on catalog
        pairs; \texttt{sd\_ratio} is realized
        residual SD vs designed.
  \item $\Dproj$ ---
        $V_{\mathrm{lin\_val}}-V_{\mathrm{lin\_lik}}$,
        oracle value cost of fitting by
        likelihood inside $\mathcal{F}$.
  \item DiD --- change in a method gap when
        $\gamma$ turns on under CRN / paired
        seeds (\emph{not} classical
        parallel-trends DiD).
  \item H1 --- pre-registered claim that
        Hybrid beats NLL under misspec.
  \item G\_well / G\_misspec --- gates:
        Hy$-$NLL should stay non-negative at
        $\gamma{=}0$, and be significantly
        positive at $\gamma{>}0$.
  \item Kish ESS $\Neff$, $\rho=\Neff/N$ ---
        effective sample size under IPS
        weights.
  \item Pinned floor --- asymptote fixed
        from oracles or huge-$N$ runs
        \emph{before} curve fitting.
  \item CRN --- common random numbers:
        same seed's embeddings, logs, and
        eval users across methods/$\gamma$.
  \item IPS / DM --- inverse-propensity
        and direct-method terms in DR
        (known $\pi_{0}$; $\hat q$ nuisance).
  \item $\alpha$, $s$, $b'(\gamma)$ ---
        click-model scale, base emb scale,
        and intercept re-solved for $5\%$
        mean CTR (Sec.~2).
\end{itemize}

%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\phonesection{1. What ``misspec'' means here}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\phonesub{Tight definition}
We study \textbf{controlled class / mean-function
misspecification} of the click (hence policy)
score relative to a locked class $\mathcal{F}$.

What stays exact: Bernoulli likelihood form,
logistic link, logging policy $\pi_{0}$, and
propensities $p=\pi_{0}(a\mid x)$.

\textbf{``Identification is not the issue''}
means: because $\pi_{0}$ is known, the
decision value $V(\pi)$ can be written in
terms of observables (via IPS / DR).  Every
failure we study is about \emph{which target
an objective converges to}, not about whether
that target is estimable in principle.

The structural issue is
\[
\ell_{\gamma}\notin\mathcal{F}
\quad\text{for }\gamma>0
\]
(and even at $\gamma{=}0$ a representation
mismatch remains --- see below).

\phonesub{Classical analogy, then our twist}
Classical MLE misspec: data from $P^{\star}$,
estimate inside a wrong family $\Rightarrow$
KL projection (pseudo-true parameter).  That
pseudo-true object need not minimize the
downstream risk you care about.

We engineer the same spirit.  We
\textbf{construct} laws $P_{\gamma}$ so that,
as $\gamma$ grows, the NLL pseudo-true policy
inside $\mathcal{F}$ becomes deliberately
poor for decision value $V$.  Then we compare
NLL to a value-oriented objective (DR/OPC)
and to a hybrid schedule.

\miniex{NLL asks the model to predict click
probabilities well on the pairs we
\emph{logged}, weighted by how often the
logger showed them.  A policy is judged only
on whether each user's top item is truly
best.  With limited capacity these goals
trade off.\\[0.3em]
Toy (not experimental): one user; items
A,B,C with true CTRs $0.20$, $0.18$, $0.01$;
logger shows C $80\%$ of the time.  NLL
spends most effort calibrating C and may
settle on $\hat q_B>\hat q_A$.  Value only
cares that A ranks above B, so a value
objective would give up C's calibration to
get that order right.\\[0.3em]
Formally: as $N\to\infty$, NLL $\to$ KL
projection of $q^{\star}$ onto $\mathcal{F}$
under the logging measure; DR moves toward
the in-class policy with highest value.
They agree only when the truth is in
$\mathcal{F}$.}

\phonesub{What this is NOT}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Not unobserved confounding ---
        $\pi_{0}$ is known.
  \item Not ``the world is misspecified'' ---
        worlds aren't; models relative to
        $\mathcal{F}$ are.
  \item Not generic deep-net misspec ---
        $\mathcal{F}$ is a small locked class.
  \item Not primarily OPE identification
        failure --- propensities are exact;
        the story is class stress on the
        policy score.
\end{itemize}

\phonesub{Three models (keep separate)}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item True logit $\ell_{\gamma}$ --- DGP.
  \item Policy score class $\mathcal{F}$ ---
        what $\pi_{\theta}$ may represent.
  \item Reward model $\hat{q}$ --- DR
        nuisance.
\end{enumerate}
Primary claim: (1) vs (2).

\phonesub{Important $\gamma{=}0$ correction}
Even the locked \texttt{high}-bias cell at
$\gamma{=}0$ is \textbf{not} fully
well-specified.  Learners see biased
embeddings ($\texttt{signal\_kept}\approx 0.5$).
Truth is bilinear in \emph{clean} embeddings,
so a representation mismatch remains.

Say: ``$\gamma{=}0$ = baseline with
representation mismatch only; $\gamma{>}0$
adds structured residual on top.''

%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\phonesection{2. Data-generating process}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\phonesub{World equation (matches code)}
\begin{align*}
\ell_{\gamma}(x,a)
  &= \underbrace{s\,\langle u_x, v_a\rangle
     + b'(\gamma)}_{\text{main taste + intercept}}
     + \gamma\,\alpha\,\phi(x,a),\\
q_{\gamma}
  &= \sigma(\ell_{\gamma}),\qquad
r\sim\mathrm{Bern}(q_{\gamma}),\\
a &\sim \pi_0(\,\cdot\mid x)
  \quad\text{(temp.\ frozen at $\gamma{=}0$)}.
\end{align*}
Here $s$ is the base env scale on the clean
dot product; $\alpha$ is the calibration
scale from the click-model solver (same
$\alpha$ that targets best-item CTR in the
$\gamma{=}0$ world); $\phi$ is unit-sd (v2)
or legacy-scaled (v1).  Code comment:
\texttt{logit = scale*<u,v> + offset'
+ gamma*alpha*phi\_std}.

\begin{itemize}
  \item $\gamma$ dials residual loudness.
  \item Family chooses the \emph{geometry}
        of $\phi$.
  \item $b'(\gamma)$ is re-solved so mean
        CTR on fixed calibration pairs stays
        $5\%$ as $\gamma$ changes.
  \item Logger temperature is frozen at the
        $\gamma{=}0$ world so logged support
        does not secretly track $\gamma$.
\end{itemize}

\phonesub{Three different CTRs (do not mix)}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Catalog-average CTR $\approx 5\%$
        (pinned by $b'$).
  \item Logger greedy CTR $\approx 9$--$11\%$
        (not re-targeted).
  \item Oracle / learned $V(\pi)$ in
        Sec.~6--7 is $13$--$39$\,pt because
        $V$ is the CTR of each user's
        \emph{single top-ranked} item, far
        above the average item.
\end{itemize}
So ``$+1.53$\,pt'' should be read against
learned values of roughly $15$--$30$\,pt,
not against $5\%$.  Raising $\gamma$ can raise
best-item CTR (and the ceiling
$V_{\mathrm{lin\_val}}$); that is why we
compare \emph{gaps}, not raw levels, across
$\gamma$.

\phonesub{Evaluation (oracle $V$, not OPE)}
\begin{equation*}
V(\pi)
  = \mathbb{E}_{x}\!\Big[
      q_{\gamma}\!\big(x,\arg\max_{a}
      \mathrm{score}_{\pi}(x,a)\big)
    \Big].
\end{equation*}
Primary tables use a simulator oracle for
$V$: which method ranks better under the
truth.  That is intentional --- not an OPE
unbiasedness claim.

\phonesub{Shared facet scaffolding}
Facets are the low-dimensional coordinates
in which every residual family is built.
Once per seed / \texttt{proj\_tag}, draw
random projections $G,H$ so each user/item
gets $m$ z-scored facet coords $t_{u,k}$,
$t_{a,k}$.  Locked: $m{=}4$,
\texttt{proj\_tag=default}, \texttt{norm=v2}
(except $\Rstar$ legacy v1).  Sharing the
draw means family differences come from the
nonlinearity, not from a lucky projection.

%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\phonesection{3. Residual families}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

All families plug into the same world
equation; only $\phi$'s geometry changes.
Careful wording: $\phi$ is constructed to be
near-orthogonal to \emph{clean} bilinear
structure; measured visibility on
\emph{biased} features is $\approx 0$
linearly --- that is a measurement, not an
automatic consequence of the construction.

\phonesub{$\Rstar$ (tanh) --- even second channel}
Build a second bilinear index from facets,
then push each facet through an even map
$\psi$ so flipping a facet's sign leaves
$\psi$ unchanged.  Linear scores are odd in
$t$; an even residual is therefore roughly
uncorrelated with them --- soft orthogonality
without an explicit projection.
\begin{align*}
\psi(t)
  &= \tanh\!\big(s_{\psi}(t^{2}-1)\big),\\
\phi_{\mathrm{raw}}
  &= m^{-1/2}\sum_{k}
     \psi(t_{u,k})\,\psi(t_{a,k}),
\end{align*}
then \textbf{double-center} (subtract user
means and item means, restore grand mean, so
$\phi$ has no pure user/item main effect
that affine terms in $\mathcal{F}$ would
absorb); legacy v1 scaling accidentally
divided both factor sides --- \textbf{not}
unit-sd.

\miniex{$\psi(2)=\psi(-2)>0$ and
$\psi(0)<0$: it asks ``is this facet extreme
or typical?'', not ``which direction?''
For a fixed item, a bilinear score flips
when the user's facet flips, but
$\psi(t_u)\psi(t_a)$ does not.  If facets
are roughly symmetric around 0, positive /
negative contributions cancel $\Rightarrow$
near-zero linear correlation with bilinear
scores.  Still a product of per-side
transforms $=$ low-rank, so richer classes
can absorb it.}

Foils: PL = same product without $\psi$;
NC1 = pure noise, amp-matched.

\phonesub{SoftAND$^{\star}$ --- thresholded AND}
Treat each facet as a soft topic light.
Bonus when the \emph{same} facet is above
threshold on \emph{both} sides.
\begin{align*}
\mathrm{sp}(z)&=\log(1+e^{z}),\\
g_{u,k}&=\mathrm{sp}\!\big((t_{u,k}-c)/s_g\big),\\
h_{a,k}&=\mathrm{sp}\!\big((t_{a,k}-c)/s_g\big),\\
\phi_{\mathrm{raw}}
  &= m^{-1/2}\sum_{k} g_{u,k}\,h_{a,k},
\end{align*}
then PERP, then unit-sd.
Locked: $m{=}4$, $c{=}1$, $s_g{=}0.25$,
\texttt{proj\_tag=default}, \texttt{norm=v2}.

\miniex{Facet ``sci-fi.''  Gates fire only
when both user and item exceed $c$ on that
facet; product $=$ soft AND.  Sum over
facets $=$ multi-topic ANDs.}

vs $\Rstar$: one-sided softplus gates, not
even $\tanh(t^{2}-1)$; explicit PERP after.
Before PERP still separable
($\mathrm{rank}\le m$).

\phonesub{SoftThresh --- soft threshold on $z_{2}$}
Ignore per-facet AND.  Take the second-index
bilinear score and bonus only when that
scalar is large.
\begin{align*}
z_{2}
  &= m^{-1/2}\sum_{k} t_{u,k}\,t_{a,k},\\
\tilde{z}_{2}
  &= (z_{2}-\mu)/\sigma,\\
\phi_{\mathrm{raw}}
  &= \mathrm{sp}\!\big(
       (\tilde{z}_{2}-\tau)/s_{\mathrm{th}}
     \big),
\end{align*}
then PERP, unit-sd.
Locked: $m{=}4$, $\tau{=}1.5$,
$s_{\mathrm{th}}{=}0.25$,
\texttt{norm=v2}.

softplus-of-a-sum is \textbf{not}
finite-rank separable --- different inductive
bias.  Larger $\tau$ $\Rightarrow$ rarer
``on'' pairs $\Rightarrow$ spikier
$\phi_{\mathrm{raw}}$ before unit-sd.

\miniex{SoftAND vs SoftThresh at $m{=}2$.
User $(2,0)$ with item $(2,0)$: SoftAND
fires on facet 1.  User $(1.2,1.2)$ with
item $(1.2,1.2)$: neither facet may clear
the sharp gate at $c{=}1$, but
$z_{2}\propto 1.2^{2}+1.2^{2}$ is large, so
SoftThresh can still fire.}

\phonesub{PERP (bilinear strip)}
\begin{equation*}
\phi
  = \mathrm{unit\_sd}\!\big(
      \phi_{\mathrm{raw}}
      - \Pi_{\mathrm{bilinear}}(\phi_{\mathrm{raw}})
    \big).
\end{equation*}
$\Pi$ regresses $\phi_{\mathrm{raw}}$ on every
affine-bilinear function of the
\emph{clean} embeddings under
\emph{uniform} catalog pairs, and keeps the
residual.  What survives is structure no
clean bilinear score can reproduce.

\miniex{Learners see \emph{biased}
embeddings and train on \emph{logged}
pairs.  A function orthogonal to the clean
span can still correlate with something in
the biased span.  That is why we always
report $R^{2}(\phi\mid\mathrm{biased})$.
See Sec.~6 for measured
$R^{2}(\phi\mid\mathrm{biased})$.}

\phonesub{Unit-SD (\texttt{norm=v2})}
SoftAND$^{\star}$/ST rescale catalog pair-SD
of $\phi$ to $1$.  $\Rstar$ v1 does not.
Consequence: SoftAND/ST track
$\mathrm{sd\_ratio}\approx\gamma$; $\Rstar$
overshoots ($\approx 2.4\times$ at
$\gamma{=}0.5$).  Same nominal $\gamma$ mixes
loudness with geometry --- prefer $\Dproj$.

\phonesub{Foils}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{PL:} same $z_{2}$ bilinear,
        unit-sd, no PERP --- ``just a second
        linear channel?''
  \item \textbf{NC1:} feature-free noise,
        \texttt{nc1\_amp}${}=0.5$ --- ``just
        any logit jitter?''
\end{itemize}

%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\phonesection{4. Learning objectives}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\phonesub{NLL (logged Bernoulli)}
\begin{equation*}
\max_{\theta}
\sum_{i}\Big[
  r_{i}\log q_{\theta}(x_{i},a_{i})
  +(1-r_{i})\log\big(1-q_{\theta}(x_{i},a_{i})\big)
\Big],
\end{equation*}
with $q_{\theta}=\sigma(\mathrm{score}_{\theta})$
and $\mathrm{score}_{\theta}\in\mathcal{F}$.

NLL fits $r\mid(x,a)$ --- supervised click
prediction on logged pairs.  It is
\textbf{not} behavior cloning of $\pi_{0}$
(which would fit $a\mid x$).  Its only link
to $\pi_{0}$ is that $\pi_{0}$ decides which
pairs get weight.

Under misspec, NLL $\to$ KL projection of
$q^{\star}$ onto $\mathcal{F}$ under the
logging measure.  That projection maximizes
likelihood, not necessarily $V$
(proxy / Goodhart).

\phonesub{DR / OPC}
OPC is the repo's doubly-robust
\emph{policy-learning} objective: a
direct-method term using $\hat{q}$ plus an
IPS correction with known $\pi_{0}$.
Training scores a stochastic policy
$\pi_{\mathrm{soft}}$ (softmax over scores);
gradients flow through DM$+$IPS.
Evaluation then uses the greedy argmax.
So ``DR targets best-in-class value'' holds
strictly in the low-temperature limit;
greedy eval is the operational target.

Pays IPS variance when $\pi$ diverges from
$\pi_{0}$.

Sketch (DM$+$IPS value surrogate; exact
code may weight/clip terms):
\begin{equation*}
\widehat{V}_{\mathrm{DR}}(\pi)
  \approx \mathbb{E}\Big[
    \hat q(x,\pi)
    + \tfrac{\pi(a\mid x)}{\pi_{0}(a\mid x)}
      \big(r-\hat q(x,a)\big)
  \Big].
\end{equation*}
\why{NLL fits logged likelihood; DR targets
decision value. Softmax train $\ne$ greedy
eval --- greedy $V$ is the reported metric.}

\phonesub{Hybrid schedule}
\begin{align*}
\text{epochs }1..fT &:\;\mathrm{NLL},\\
\text{epochs }fT..T &:\;\mathrm{DR\ fine\text{-}tune},\\
f_{\star} &= 0.5\quad\text{(locked)}.
\end{align*}

\miniex{With fixed budget $T{=}20$: $f{=}1$
is pure NLL; $f{=}0$ is pure DR; $f{=}0.5$
means 10 NLL epochs then 10 DR epochs
\emph{from those weights}.  Total compute
is the same for every $f$ --- hence
``objective schedule,'' not a compute dial.
Hypothesis: NLL quickly reaches a stable
low-variance region near the likelihood
projection; DR then moves ranking toward
value without starting cold, where IPS
variance hurts most.  $f{=}0.5$ was locked
on a 3-seed pilot --- treat as
pre-registered choice, not an optimum.}

%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\phonesection{5. Estimands}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\begin{align*}
D(\gamma)
  &= V(\mathrm{OPC},\gamma)
   - V(\mathrm{NLL},\gamma)
   \quad\text{(level)},\\
D_{H}(\gamma)
  &= V(\mathrm{Hy},\gamma)
   - V(\mathrm{NLL},\gamma)
   \quad\text{(level)},\\
\mathrm{DiD}(\gamma)
  &= D(\gamma)-D(0)
   \quad\text{(interaction)}.
\end{align*}

\textbf{DiD here is a designed interaction,
not classical DiD.}  Picture a $2\times 2$
with methods (OPC, NLL) as rows and worlds
($\gamma{=}0$, $\gamma{=}0.5$) as columns.
$D(\gamma)$ $=$ row difference within a
column; DiD $=$ how that difference changes
between columns.  We set $\gamma$ ourselves;
both methods run on the same seed (same
embeddings, logs, eval users $=$ CRN,
common random numbers).  The
interaction is identified by design; pairing
cancels shared seed noise.

\miniex{If $D(0)\approx -1.07$ and
$\mathrm{DiD}=+1.53$, then
$D(0.5)\approx +0.46$ (same run family).
OPC loses slightly in the baseline and wins
once the residual is on.  Do \emph{not}
confuse this DiD with the nearby Hy$-$NLL
\emph{level} $+1.54$ at $\Rstar$ $\gamma0.5$
--- different method pair.}

Oracle projection gaps (no learning):
\begin{align*}
\Dproj(\gamma)
  &= V_{\mathrm{lin\_val}}(\gamma)
   - V_{\mathrm{lin\_lik}}(\gamma),\\
\DDproj(\gamma)
  &= \Dproj(\gamma)-\Dproj(0).
\end{align*}

\begin{itemize}
  \item $V_{\mathrm{lin\_val}}$ --- best
        greedy value any affine-bilinear
        ranker can reach under true $q$
        (clean features / value objective).
  \item $V_{\mathrm{lin\_lik}}$ --- value of
        the bilinear ranker that maximizes
        expected likelihood (NLL's putative
        asymptote \emph{if} the pin were
        correct --- Sec.~8.4 says it is not).
\end{itemize}
Exact pair measure / feature details of each
oracle: verify against the recoverability
code before citing as asymptotes.

\why{Oracle gaps bound what is possible;
they do not predict what learners realize.
At $\gamma{=}0$, $\Dproj\approx 11$\,pt but
empirical $D\approx -1.07$: finite-sample
variance, optimization, and selection eat
the difference.  Scaling therefore uses
$\DDprojeff$, not raw $\DDproj$.}

\textbf{DiD identifies:} how the method gap
moves when residual turns on, protocol fixed.
\textbf{Does not identify:} a unique field
``misspec parameter,'' or external validity
beyond this DGP.

%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\phonesection{6. Recoverability \stag{OBSERVED}}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\textbf{Question.} How much of $\phi$ / true
logit is visible from the learner's
\emph{biased} features?

\begin{align*}
\texttt{signal\_kept}

\phonesection{8. Scaling laws --- method}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\stag{PLANNED} / \stag{HYPOTHESIS} for
exponents; Sec.~7c already \stag{OBSERVED}
for qualitative $N^{\star}$ / capacity.

\textbf{Method, not a fitted result.}  What we
measure, how we pin floors, which curves we
fit, what counts as support or refute.

\phonesub{8.0 Six steps}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Nested train prefixes A1 (one pool
        per seed; prefixes; val fixed 20k).
  \item Matched $\{\mathrm{NLL},\mathrm{OPC},
        \mathrm{Hy}_{0.5}\}$ at each $N$
        (same HPO; select on val DR).
  \item Record oracle $V$, learned $V$,
        ESS/$\rho$ for DR.
  \item Validate / redefine floors (A3)
        \emph{before} any curve fit.
  \item Fit crossover $N_{\star}(\gamma)$ on
        held-in $N$; check it falls as
        $\DDprojeff$ rises.
  \item Predict held-out largest $N$; else
        ``trends only''.
\end{enumerate}

\phonesub{8.1 Question}
Under locked class misspec, how do
$D$ and $\mathrm{DiD}(\gamma)$ move when
scarce resources ($N$, ESS) and capacity $C$
change --- bias, $\mathcal{F}$, family,
$f_{\star}$ fixed?  Target: curves $+$
crossover points, not one universal
exponent.

\phonesub{8.2 Why not Kaplan / Chinchilla}
Logged NLL $\ne$ eval target under misspec
(also at locked $\gamma{=}0$: representation
mismatch).  Floors are stacked (features /
class / objective), not one $E$.  Binding
axes: $N$, ESS, HPO --- not FLOPs.  $\gamma$
is a stress dial, not a resource.

Still borrow: reducible regret to a pinned
oracle; three regimes; leave-largest-$N$-out;
matched HPO at every scale point.

\phonesub{8.3 Axes and data unit}
Design axis $=N$ (logged rows).  ESS is
endogenous for DR:
\begin{align*}
w_{i}
  &= \frac{\pi_{\mathrm{soft}}(a_{i}\mid x_{i})}
         {\pi_{0}(a_{i}\mid x_{i})},\\
\Neff
  &= \frac{(\sum_{i} w_{i})^{2}}
         {\sum_{i} w_{i}^{2}},\qquad
\rho=\Neff/N.
\end{align*}
For NLL set $\Neff:=N$.  Fit primary curves
vs $N$; also report DR vs $\Neff$ (claim S6).
One design per fit --- do not mix
$N$, $N/|A|$, $N/|U|$.

\phonesub{8.4 Pin floor, then fit excess}
\begin{align*}
R_{m}
  &= V_{\mathrm{ref}}(C,\gamma)
   - V_{m}(N,C,\gamma),\\
V_{\mathrm{ref}}
  &= \max\!\big(
       V_{\mathrm{lin\_val}}(\gamma),\,
       V_{\mathrm{repair},C}(\gamma)
     \big),\\
B_{m}
  &:= V_{\mathrm{ref}}-V_{m}^{\infty}
  \quad\text{(A3)},\\
R_{m}-B_{m}
  &= a_{m}\,N^{-\kappa_{m}}
  \quad\text{(primary; vs $N$)}.
\end{align*}
Optional DR diagnostic replaces $N$ by
$\Neff$ (for NLL they coincide).  Fit only
$(a_{m},\kappa_{m})$ on excess --- floor is
pinned, not free.

\textbf{\stag{ISSUE} (blocks fitting).}
At $\gamma0$, learned NLL already reaches
$\approx 18$\,pt $> V_{\mathrm{lin\_lik}}\,13.05$
(same at $\Rstar$ $\gamma2$: $16.81>13.88$).
A learner cannot beat its own asymptote, so
the lik-oracle is not NLL's pseudo-true
target.  Candidates (unverified): (a) oracle
projects under uniform pairs, real NLL under
logging measure; (b) early stopping /
val-DR selection steers away from pure
likelihood; (c) feature mismatch.  A3
(huge-$N$ NLL) replaces the theoretical pin
with an empirical one.  No exponent until
then.

\phonesub{8.5 Regret stack (ledger)}
Accounting identity
($\Delta_{\mathrm{feat}}$: feature/bias map;
$\Delta_{\mathrm{class}}$: capacity of
$\mathcal{F}$; $\Delta_{\mathrm{obj},m}$:
objective gap vs value; $\varepsilon$:
estimation / HPO noise):
\begin{align*}
V_{\mathrm{ceil}}-V_{m}
  &= \Delta_{\mathrm{feat}}
   + \Delta_{\mathrm{class}}
   + \Delta_{\mathrm{obj},m}
   + \varepsilon_{\mathrm{est},m}.
\end{align*}
If feature and class terms are the same for
OPC and NLL (matched $C$, matched HPO), they
cancel in the difference:
\begin{align*}
D
  &= \Dprojeff-\Delta_{\varepsilon},\\
\Dprojeff
  &:= \Delta_{\mathrm{obj},\mathrm{NLL}}
    - \Delta_{\mathrm{obj},\mathrm{DR}},\\
\DDprojeff(\gamma)
  &:= \Dprojeff(\gamma)-\Dprojeff(0).
\end{align*}
If methods select different widths or HPO
winners, cancellation fails --- fix the
protocol, do not narrate a negative ledger
term as a finding.

Loss-scale heuristic (not value-scale;
omitted-variable intuition):
\begin{equation*}
B_{\mathrm{repr},L}
  \propto (\gamma\alpha)^{2}\,\mathrm{Var}(\phi)\,
  \big[1-R^{2}(\phi\mid\mathrm{biased})\big].
\end{equation*}

\phonesub{8.6 Law 1: crossover}
\stag{HYPOTHESIS}

DR pays IPS variance to cut projection bias.
NLL is lower-variance but wrong-target under
misspec.  As $N$ grows, DR variance shrinks
while the bias gap stays $\Rightarrow$
crossover $N_{\star}$ where
$D(\gamma;N_{\star})=0$ at matched $C$.

Prefer DiD / difference forms over a ratio
(sign-fragile near 0).  Prefer
$\DDprojeff$ over raw $\DDproj$ (Sec.~5).

\textbf{Test:} nested A1; methods
$\{\mathrm{NLL},\mathrm{OPC},\mathrm{Hy}_{0.5}\}$;
HPO 20 (A4 checks 60); select on val DR;
$N\in\{25\mathrm{k},\ldots,800\mathrm{k}\}$ then
$1.6$M; worlds locked-high $\gamma{=}0$ plus
$\gamma\in\{0.5,2\}\times\{\Rstar,\mathrm{ST}\}$;
leave-largest-$N$-out first.

\phonesub{8.7 Law 2: capacity gate}
\stag{HYPOTHESIS} --- secondary; unsettled

Extra width helps only if the class can see
residual structure on \textbf{biased}
features; else $C$ buys IPS noise.

\textbf{Gate (incremental):}
\begin{equation*}
\mathrm{Gate}
  = R^{2}_{\mathrm{tt}}(\ell\mid\mathrm{biased})
  - R^{2}_{\mathrm{lin}}(\ell\mid\mathrm{biased}).
\end{equation*}
tt $=$ two-tower / separable nonlinear class.
\textbf{Not} $R^{2}(\phi\mid\mathrm{biased})$
alone.

\miniex{Illustrative (not measured): at one

\phonesub{8.9 Claims S1--S7}

        DiD vs $\log N$.  A pure
        finite-sample artifact would shrink
        ($\beta_{\mathrm{DiD}}<0$).
        Lower bound of $\beta_{\mathrm{DiD}}>0$
        is the stronger ``grows / persists''
        claim.
  \item S2 under misspec, NLL saturates or
        inverts vs $\gamma{=}0$ / well-spec.
  \item S3 $N_{\star}$ falls with
        $\DDprojeff$.

\end{itemize}
