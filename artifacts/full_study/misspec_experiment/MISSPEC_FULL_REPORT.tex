\phonesection{2. Math}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\phonesub{2.1 World}
\[
\ell_\gamma(u,a)
=\alpha\bigl(\tilde z_1(u,a)+\gamma\,\phi(u,a)\bigr)
+b'(\gamma),
\quad \mathrm{sd}(\phi)=1.
\]
\(\tilde z_1\): biased BPR bilinear
(frozen emb).
\(\phi\): residual after double-center /
PERP (strip clean affine-bilinear) /
unit-sd.
Logger \(\pi_0\) temperature frozen at
\(\gamma{=}0\) and reused (CRN).
Reference CTR \(5\%\).

\why{Click logit $=$ biased taste $+$
controlled residual; logger frozen so
only \(\phi\)/ \(\gamma\) change the world.}

\phonesub{2.2 Objectives}
NLL: KL projection of \(\pi_0\) onto
\(\mathcal{F}\) (fit logged pairs).
OPC: DR estimate of \textbf{greedy}
\(V(\pi)\) inside \(\mathcal{F}\).
Hybrid \(f\): NLL for fraction \(f\) of epochs,
then DR fine-tune (\(f^\star{=}0.5\)).

\phonesub{2.3 Estimands}
\[
D(\gamma)=V(\pi^{\mathrm{OPC}}_\gamma)-V(\pi^{\mathrm{NLL}}_\gamma),
\quad
\DiD=D(\gamma^\star)-D(0).
\]
\(N^\star(\gamma,C)\): first train size with
mean \(D\le 0\) for class \(C\).
Oracle gap \(\Delta_{\mathrm{proj}}=
V_{\mathrm{lin,val}}-V_{\mathrm{lin,lik}}\)
bounds what likelihood vs value prefer
inside \(\mathcal{F}\).

\why{Level \(D\) \(\neq\) DiD --- quote which.
Greedy \(V\) is deployment; stochastic \(V\)
is not the primary metric.}

%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\phonesection{3. Misspec types (families)}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

Shared: facet scores \(t_{u,k},t_{a,k}\) from
random projections; then \(\phi\) from a map.

\phonesub{\(\Rstar\) (primary)}
Even warp then product:
\(\psi(t)=\tanh(s(t^2-1))\),
\(\phi_{\mathrm{raw}}\propto\sum_k\psi(t_{u,k})\psi(t_{a,k})\).
Evenness \(\Rightarrow\) near-orthogonal to
linear scores. Spec: \(s{=}1,m{=}4\).

\phonesub{SoftAND\(^\star\)}
Thresholded co-activation:
\(g=\mathrm{softplus}((t_u-c)/s)\),
\(h=\mathrm{softplus}((t_a-c)/s)\),
\(\phi_{\mathrm{raw}}\propto\sum_k g_k h_k\),
then \(\perp\) bilinear span.
``AND-like'' facet concordance.

\phonesub{SoftThresh}
Threshold on the \emph{scalar} second index
\(z_2\propto\sum_k t_{u,k}t_{a,k}\):
\(\phi_{\mathrm{raw}}=\mathrm{softplus}((z_2-\tau)/s)\),
then \(\perp\).
Sparse high-\(z_2\) spikes; not finite-rank
separable.

\phonesub{Foils}
PL: second bilinear, no \(\perp\) (placebo
channel). NC1: feature-free noise
(\(\mathrm{sd}\) matched). Cosine:
angles only (exploratory; often absorbed).

\why{Do not pool families. SoftAND mid-\(\gamma\)
\(\neq\) SoftAND \(\gamma{=}100\)
(\(\phi\)-dominated regime).}

\phonesection{6. Scaling, capacity, ESS}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

Nested \(N\)-ladders (ML, \(\Rstar\), \(n{=}5\),
clip:10). Absolute \(V_{\mathrm{OPC}}\)
\textbf{rises} with \(N\) always. Mild \(\gamma\):
\(D\) flips as NLL catches projection.
