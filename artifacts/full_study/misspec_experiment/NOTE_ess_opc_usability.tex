\section*{Claim}
OPC beats NLL when misspecification is strong
\emph{relative to} sample size $N$ and the policy class---
\textbf{not} because DR ``gets more ESS.''

\[
D(N)
=
100\cdot\bigl(
V_{\mathrm{greedy}}(\mathrm{OPC})
-
V_{\mathrm{greedy}}(\mathrm{NLL})
\bigr)
\quad\text{(CTR percentage points).}
\]
$D>0$ means OPC is ahead.

\section*{What we ran}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{R$^\star$ linear ladder} done:
  $6.25$k$\to 400$k, $\gamma\in\{0,0.5,2\}$ + wellspec.
\item \textbf{Capacity $\times N$} Tier~1 done
  (\texttt{linear+mlp}, $H{=}256$);
  Tier~2 densify in flight
  ($g0.5$ 50/200k done; $g2$ running).
\item \textbf{Skipped}: SoftAND/ST $N$-ladders.
\end{itemize}

\section*{What is ``large $N$''?}
Not raw $N$. On ML ($|U|{=}6038$, $|A|{=}3533$,
frozen $d{=}32$): at $N{=}400$k,
linear ($p{\approx}2112$) has $N/p\sim 190$;
mlp256 ($p{\approx}35$k) has $N/p\sim 11$.
$N$ is large once $N\gtrsim N^\star(\gamma,C)$.
Mechanism generalizes; the numbers do not.

\section*{ESS is not the flip}
Under misspec $\mathrm{ESS}/N$ collapses while
ranking still flips (mild) or grows (strong, linear).
Wellspec keeps high $\mathrm{ESS}/N$ \emph{and} $D\to 0$.
Binding axis: \textbf{misspec vs class}.

\section*{Rule of thumb}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Well-spec $+$ large $N$: NLL and OPC converge.
\item Mild misspec: OPC helps early; crossover
  earlier with more capacity ($\sim$50k mlp vs
  $\sim$100--200k linear).
\item Strong misspec vs a \emph{small} class:
  OPC stays ahead at large $N$; a wider class
  can absorb most of that edge.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Headline}
\small
Usability is class-relative.
Mild misspec $\Rightarrow$ OPC helps early then converges
(sooner with capacity);
strong misspec $\Rightarrow$ OPC stays ahead at large $N$
only if the class cannot absorb it.
ESS clipping is not what flips the ranking.
