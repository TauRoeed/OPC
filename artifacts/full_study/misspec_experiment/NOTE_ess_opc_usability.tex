\section*{Why ``OPC loses with \(N\)'' is wrong}
OPC's \emph{absolute} greedy CTR rises with \(N\).
What flips is \(D=V_{\mathrm{OPC}}-V_{\mathrm{NLL}}\).
Mild misspec: NLL catches an absorbable projection.
Strong \(\gamma\): NLL stalls, OPC keeps climbing.

\section*{Headline}
OPC does not degrade with \(N\); NLL catches absorbable misspec.
Strong misspec vs small class \(\Rightarrow\) NLL stalls, OPC rises.
Decisions: \textbf{greedy}. CIs: seed-level 95\% \(t\).
