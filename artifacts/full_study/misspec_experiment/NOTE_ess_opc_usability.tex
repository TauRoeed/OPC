\section*{Claim}
OPC beats NLL when misspecification is strong
\emph{relative to} sample size $N$ and the policy class---
\textbf{not} because DR ``gets more ESS.''

\[
D(N)
=
100\cdot\bigl(
V_{\mathrm{greedy}}(\mathrm{OPC})
-
V_{\mathrm{greedy}}(\mathrm{NLL})
\bigr)
\quad\text{(CTR percentage points).}
\]
$D>0$ means OPC is ahead.

\section*{What we ran}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{R$^\star$ ladder} done for $\gamma\in\{0,0.5,2\}$:
  nested sizes $6.25$k$\to 400$k,
  tags \texttt{scale\_p1\_rstar\_g*},
  methods nll / opc / hybrid\_seq\_f0.5,
  5 seeds, 60 Optuna trials,
  \texttt{clip:10}, linear policy.
\item \textbf{Wellspec fill}: stage-1 done; densify in flight
  (\texttt{scale\_p1\_wellspec}).
\item \textbf{Skipped}: SoftAND/ST $N$-ladders
  (use hybrid $\gamma$-grid @ $100$k instead).
\item \textbf{Deferred}: $800$k (breaks $N_{\max}{=}400$k pool).
\end{itemize}

\medskip
\textbf{Crossover} $N^\star(\gamma)=\min\{N:\,D(N)\le 0\}$:
\begin{itemize}
\item $\gamma=0$: $N^\star\in(25\mathrm{k},50\mathrm{k}]$
\item $\gamma=0.5$: $N^\star\in(100\mathrm{k},200\mathrm{k}]$
\item $\gamma=2$: \textbf{no crossover} $\le 400\mathrm{k}$
  ($D$ widens; at 400k, OPC$\approx 21\%$, NLL$\approx 15\%$)
\item wellspec (stage-1): $D$: $+6.8\to +1.8\to +0.3$
  as $N$: $25\mathrm{k}\to 100\mathrm{k}\to 400\mathrm{k}$
\end{itemize}

\section*{ESS is not the flip}
Winning-run weight ESS under misspec grows slowly while
$\mathrm{ESS}/N$ collapses (e.g.\ $R^\star\,\gamma{=}2$:
$\sim\!1\%$ at 25k $\to$ $0.26\%$ at 400k).
Wellspec keeps $\mathrm{ESS}/N\sim 12$--$15\%$ \emph{and}
$D\to 0$.
So ranking tracks \textbf{misspec vs class}, not ESS accumulation.

\section*{Other families (hybrid @ $N=100\mathrm{k}$)}
Min $\gamma$ with mean $D>0$ on ML:
$R^\star\gtrsim 0.75$, SoftThresh $\gtrsim 2$, SoftAND $\gtrsim 4$.
Same story; family only shifts the threshold.

\section*{Rule of thumb (linear class)}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Well-spec / absorbable misspec $+$ large $N$:
  NLL and OPC \textbf{converge}.
\item Slight misspec ($R^\star\,\gamma\approx 0.5$):
  OPC helps at small $N$, then the gap shrinks / crosses.
\item Strong misspec ($R^\star\,\gamma\gtrsim 2$):
  they do \textbf{not} converge $\le 400\mathrm{k}$;
  NLL stalls, OPC stays ahead
  (MLP width at fixed $N=100\mathrm{k}$ does not erase this).
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Headline}
\small
With a small policy class: mild misspec $\Rightarrow$ OPC helps early,
then converges; strong misspec $\Rightarrow$ OPC stays ahead at large $N$.
ESS clipping limits OPE precision; it is not what flips the ranking.
