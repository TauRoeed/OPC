\section*{Claim}
OPC beats NLL when misspec is strong
\emph{relative to} \(N\) and the policy class---
not because DR ``gets more ESS.''
Full tables with 95\% \(t\) CIs:
\texttt{NOTE\_ess\_opc\_usability.md}.

\section*{Why ``OPC loses with \(N\)'' is wrong}
\textbf{OPC's absolute greedy CTR rises with \(N\).}
What flips is the gap \(D=V_{\mathrm{OPC}}-V_{\mathrm{NLL}}\).

{\small
\begin{tabular}{@{}l rr@{}}
\toprule
world (linear) & OPC 6.25k$\to$400k & NLL 6.25k$\to$400k \\
\midrule
$\gamma{=}0.5$ & $14.3\to 19.3$ & $11.4\to 19.5$ \\
$\gamma{=}2$   & $14.1\to 21.0$ & $10.6\to 15.4$ \\
wellspec       & $28.9\to 29.6$ & $19.0\to 29.4$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}}

\medskip
Mild / wellspec: NLL catches an absorbable projection
$\Rightarrow D\to 0$.
Strong $\gamma$: NLL stalls, OPC keeps climbing
$\Rightarrow D$ stays large.
Clipped ESS$/N$ collapses, so more rows help NLL's
parametric fit more than OPC's OPE selector.

\section*{Greedy vs stochastic}
\textbf{Greedy} (\texttt{policy\_rewards\_greedy}):
deployment CTR --- primary.
\textbf{Stochastic} (\texttt{policy\_rewards}):
softmax CTR.
OPC is nearly deterministic (greedy$\approx$stoch);
NLL is diffuse (stoch $\ll$ greedy).
Stochastic $D$ is mostly a sharpness artifact.

\section*{Headline}
\small
OPC does not degrade with \(N\); NLL catches absorbable misspec.
Strong misspec vs a small class $\Rightarrow$ NLL stalls, OPC rises.
Use greedy for decisions; ESS clip is not the ranking flip.
