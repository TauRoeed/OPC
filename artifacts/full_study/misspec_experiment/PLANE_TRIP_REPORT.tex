\begin{itemize}

\phonesection{3. Ladders (greedy; abs + \(D\))}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

OPC does not degrade with \(N\): absolute
\(V_{\mathrm{OPC}}\) rises in every world.
Mild \(\gamma\): \(D\) flips as NLL catches an
absorbable projection. Strong \(\gamma{=}2\):
NLL stalls \(\approx 15\%\) @400k, OPC
\(\approx 21\%\), \(D{+}5.6\) [1.9,9.2]
\clearwin\ OPC. Clipped ESS/\(N\) collapses
while \(D\) stays \(+\) at \(\gamma{=}2\) --- ESS
does not track the sign of \(D\).

%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
\phonesection{4. Stochastic vs Gini}
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\begin{itemize}
  \item Stochastic \(D\) \clearwin\ OPC almost
        everywhere because OPC is near-
        deterministic (\texttt{logit\_scale}
        \(\sim\)20--80) while NLL stays soft
        (\(\sim\)0.2--0.4).
  \item IW Gini is high for \emph{both}
        (\(\sim\)0.90--0.98); wellspec can even
        show higher NLL Gini at small \(N\).
  \item \(\Rightarrow\) Gini is a diagnostic;
        deployment metric $=$ greedy.
\end{itemize}
