\begin{abstract}
\noindent
Locked confirmatory numbers for the misspecification dial study on \textbf{original full catalogs}
(MovieLens-1M, Anime, Myket; KuaiRec/KuaiRand Phase-0a status noted).
Primary estimand:
\[
D(\gamma)=V(\pi^{\mathrm{OPC/DR}}_{\gamma})-V(\pi^{\mathrm{NLL}}_{\gamma}),
\qquad
\DiD=D(\gstar)-D(0),
\]
with exact-greedy CTR in percentage points.
We report absolute CTR levels and DiDs for residual family \(\Rstar\), hybrid NLL\(\to\)DR,
latent SoftAND\(^\star\)/SoftThresh, and CausE/BLOB references.
Catalog-geometry interventions (mlsize / amp / damp / head-match) are \textbf{out of scope}
for this note.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setup (reminder)}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em,itemsep=0.2em]
  \item Truth logit: affine-bilinear clean score \(+\,\gamma\,\alpha\,\phi\) with \(\mathrm{sd}(\phi)=1\);
        logger temperature frozen at \(\gamma=0\); reference CTR \(5\%\).
  \item Primary family \(\Rstar\): even map on a second random projection
        (\(s{=}1,m{=}4,\psi{=}\tanh\)).
  \item Phase-1 learners: matched linear capacity; methods OPC/DR, NLL, optional
        \(\texttt{hybrid\_seq\_f0.5}\), \(n{=}10^5\) train / \(2{\cdot}10^4\) val, 20 Optuna trials.
  \item Bias cell: combined-high. Embeddings: \texttt{BPR/embeddings} (full).
\end{itemize}

\section{Takeaways}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=0.25em]
  \item \(\Rstar\) on ML: confirmatory DR advantage \(\DiD{=}+1.53\) pt is solid.
  \item Hybrid \(f{=}0.5\) is the practical interpolant: best absolute CTR under mid misspec on ML,
        and the only clear DiD win on anime/myket.
  \item CausE is overtaken by OPC-mlp in DiD under \(\Rstar\); BLOB keeps a level edge.
  \item Latent SoftAND/SoftThresh: oracle-active, learner-null at operational \(\gamma\);
        stress \(\gamma{=}100\) recovers large DiDs.
  \item Kuai full-catalog learner results: pending (Phase-0a failed gates; confirmatory still in flight).
\end{enumerate}
